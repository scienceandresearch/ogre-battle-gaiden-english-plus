# caps6: compact capitals, 6 rows per glyph, for the small pre-drawn status
# tags (tools/gfx/tags.py: the "deployed" tag of the organize status pane and
# the battle paralysis label). Original design for this project, drawn pixel by
# pixel; nothing is traced or copied from the game's art or from another font.
# Licence: MIT (the project licence, see LICENSE). The project owner may also
# dedicate it to the public domain (CC0), like assets/font/vwf8.txt.

# Metrics: every glyph is a capital (or mark) 6 px tall, rows 0-5, no
# descenders. Strokes are 1 px wide. Most letters are 3 px wide; I is 1 px,
# L is 2 px (so that FIELDED is exactly 24 px, the width of the deployed tag's
# lettering area), M and W are 5 px. N is an arch (a 3-px N with its diagonal
# in the middle column reads like M). The advance is the width + 1: the gap
# column takes the tag's right shadow, or the outline between two letters of
# the paralysis label.
# Notes: docs/workstreams/gfx-tags.md.

# Syntax (as in assets/font/vwf8.txt): '@NAME [advance=N]' starts a glyph,
# followed by 6 rows of '#' (ink) and '.' (blank), all of the same width.
# A comment line starts with '# ' and must never consist of '#' and '.' only.
