# logo19: heavy capitals for the main title of the title screen (19 rows).
# Original design for this project, drawn pixel by pixel in the manner of a
# brush-lettered logo: square stems, rounded bowls, a slanted counter in O and
# Q, tapered bar ends on E, F and T, pointed A. Nothing is traced or copied from
# the game's art or from any other font.
# Licence: MIT (the project licence, see LICENSE). The project owner may also
# dedicate it to the public domain (CC0), like vwf8.txt.

# Metrics: every glyph uses all 19 rows (cap height 19 px, no descenders).
# Stems are 3 px wide, the top and bottom bars 3 rows, the middle bars 3 rows
# (rows 8-10; B, R 7-9; G 9-11; A 11-13). Widths: I 4; E F L 7; J T Z 8; most
# letters 9; O Q 10; M W 11. Letters sit 1 px apart (the advance is the width
# + 1) and words 4 px apart (SPACE advance 3): the title's 1-px black outline
# (4 neighbours) separates them. Counters are 1-5 px wide; the outline fills
# narrow ones black, wider ones show the glow inside.
# The title renders the ink in a mottled fire or white fill (plane 1) and
# draws the black outline and a cyan/blue glow around it (plane 2)
# (tools/gfx/title.py).

# Syntax (as in vwf8.txt): '@NAME [advance=N]' starts a glyph, followed by 19
# rows of '#' (ink) and '.' (blank), all of the same width. The advance
# defaults to the width + 1. A comment line starts with '# ' and must never
# consist of '#' and '.' only.
