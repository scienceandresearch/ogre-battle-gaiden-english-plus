# Intro narration: the text crawl after power-on, before the title screen.
#
# Drawn by tools/gfx/intro.py in the title16 font (assets/font/title16.txt),
# white with a blue outline like the original lettering. One text line per
# line of the crawl, at most 144 px wide with the outline (check with
# python -m tools.gfx.intro fit). A blank line separates paragraphs. Three
# paragraphs of 6, 6 and 3 lines keep the original layout and timing (written
# in place); any other structure selects the relocated layout, which adds
# about 4.3 seconds per extra line.
#
# Terms: "true king" as in the dialogue; "Imperial Year 15" is the date in
# the Imperial Calendar (glossary.md). The ellipsis is one character (U+2026),
# the quotes are curly.

To take all the suffering
of the people upon oneself,
and to bear one's own
suffering alone…
Whoever can do this
is indeed a “true king”…

But…
can such a way of life
be called happiness?
And to force such a life
upon others…
is that truly right?

Imperial Year 15. Our story
begins in Estoa, a village
on a small peninsula…
