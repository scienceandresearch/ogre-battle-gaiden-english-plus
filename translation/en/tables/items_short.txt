# Style (as the attack names in ui.txt: ThndFlar, SncBlade, PtroBrth):
# * Drop the spaces and keep a capital on each word (CamelCase); drop "of",
#   "to" and "'s"; "X of Y" becomes "YX" (Ring of Power -> PowerRng).
# * Shorten as little as needed. Keep the word that identifies the item
#   whole where possible and shorten the generic one; recurring words always
#   take the same short form:
#     Sword Swd, Blade Blde, Spear Spr, Lance Lnc, Staff Stf, Shield Shld,
#     Ring Rng, Stone Stn, Charm Chrm, Crown Crwn, Mantle Mntl, Garb Grb,
#     Armor Armr, Fang Fng, Pumpkin Pmpk, Thunder Thnd, Dragon (Dragoon)
#     Drgn, Death Dth, Devil Dvl, Black (Jet-Black) Blk, White Wht, Silver
#     Slvr (Slv before a whole word: SlvTiara), Flame Flm, Battle Btl,
#     Mystic Myst, Samurai Samu, Down Dn; Light Lite and Night Nite (as
#     Starlite and Nitemare in ui.txt).
# * Other long words lose inner vowels, then doubled letters (Potn, Rtrn,
#   Tkt, Sumns, Scnt, Whsp, Scmtr), or take a natural shorter word (Golden
#   Gold, Burning Burn, Purified Pure, Vestment Vest, Helmet Helm, Maiden
#   Maid, Dowsing Dowse, Mountains Mountain, Archangel Angl).
# * Where abbreviating both words would hide the item, its proper name or
#   key word stands alone: Musashi, Kusanagi, Mikado, Benkei, Ozrick,
#   Kokuou, Iskender, Osafune, Kotodama, Overlord, Martyr, Mirror, Rainbow,
#   Goddess, Twinkle, FullMoon, 7-League.
# * Spell items reuse the attack names' short forms (PtroBrth, Lightnng,
#   Nitemare).
